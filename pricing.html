Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a setup fee?

No. Every Remtoo plan includes setup, platform configuration, and onboarding at no additional cost.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time, with no long-term commitment.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes. You can move between plans as your school and active student count change.

Is training included?

Yes. Guided onboarding and training resources are included so your team can launch confidently.

How many teachers can I add?

You can add the teachers your school needs. We will help configure the right access for your plan.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit cards and can arrange invoice payment for qualifying annual plans.
